  • 2026Shipped

    How the hero was made

    From scanned paper cutouts to an infinite canvas city.

    The skyline is cut from paper and scanned, then tinted to the palette at build time with its texture and shadow baked in. Seven layers move at their own depth with the pointer and the scroll, all driven by one requestAnimationFrame loop that sleeps when nothing moves. In front walks a linocut crowd by Clara Hidalgo: 30 figures in one sprite atlas, drawn on a canvas. Tap one and it jumps. With prefers-reduced-motion everything holds still.
